    Privacy

    If you sign in with Google, TasteRank gives you a random user ID. Your home country, ratings and approvals are saved against that ID, never your name. Other people only ever see totals, never who rated what.

    Your Google details: so you can sign in again, our sign-in service keeps the email address, name and profile photo Google shares when you sign in. TasteRank doesn't show them, share them or use them to contact you.

    Visitor statistics are counted without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

    Your country is guessed from your internet address to pick which snacks to show first. It isn't stored.

    Feedback you send is emailed to the TasteRank team. Add your email only if you'd like a reply.

    Product photos come from Open Food Facts, which sees your internet address when your browser loads them.

    You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time from your profile.
