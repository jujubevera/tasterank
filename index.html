How TasteRank works

    Tell us what you think

    TasteRank is brand new. Honest feedback decides what gets built next.

    Privacy

    If you sign in with Google or Facebook, your ratings and approvals are saved under a random code, never your name. Other people only ever see totals, never who rated what.

    Where you live is optional. If you tell us, it's saved with your ratings so we can compare tastes between places. We only ever show totals, never who rated what.

    Your Google or Facebook details: so you can sign in again, our sign-in service keeps the email address, name and profile photo Google or Facebook shares when you sign in. TasteRank doesn't show them, share them or use them to contact you.

    Visitor statistics are counted without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

    Your country is guessed from your internet address to pick which snacks to show first. It isn't stored.

    Feedback you send is emailed to the TasteRank team. Add your email only if you'd like a reply.

    A new subcategory you suggest when adding a snack is emailed to the TasteRank team to check, with that random code (never your name or email), so repeats from one person can be spotted. Everyone can add up to 5 snacks a day.

    Snack photos come from Open Food Facts, which sees your internet address when your browser loads them.

    Looking snacks up: When a snack isn't on TasteRank yet, what you type in the add search is also sent to Open Food Facts to look it up. Snack photos already load from there.

    You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time from your profile.
