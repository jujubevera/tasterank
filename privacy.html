Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

What TasteRank is: a free community site where people rate snack flavours. You can browse without an account; you only sign in (with Google) to rate, add or approve snacks.

If you sign in, we store your Google name, email address, home country, and the ratings and votes you give, in our Supabase database. Other people only ever see totals, never who rated what.

Visitor statistics are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

Your country is guessed from your internet address (via country.is) to pick which foods to show first. It isn't stored.

Feedback you send is emailed to the TasteRank team via Web3Forms.

Other services: the page loads its font from Google Fonts, its database code from jsDelivr, and product photos from Open Food Facts. Like any website, they see your internet address when your browser fetches these files.

You can delete your account and everything linked to it at any time from your profile.

What we don't do: no advertising, no selling or sharing your data, no tracking cookies.

Questions or deletion requests: use the Feedback button on tasterank.app, or delete your account yourself from your profile.
